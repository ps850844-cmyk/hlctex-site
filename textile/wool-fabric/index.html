Why choose HLC for wool fabrics?

More than a decade of local wool experience

HLC and leading wool yarn supplier Xinao are both based in Tongxiang, Jiaxing. More than ten years of cooperation have given our team extensive experience in Merino wool yarn selection and wool knit fabric development.

Competitive pricing and dependable lead times

Close access to the local wool supply chain shortens sourcing and communication time, helping HLC offer competitive pricing, consistent quality control and reliable delivery.

Responsible Merino wool options

Depending on project requirements, HLC can develop RWS-certified wool, mulesing-free wool, ZQ Merino wool and performance wool blends for premium apparel.
