Product information

This lightweight 120GSM single jersey blends 17.5 micron fine merino wool with 37.5® performance polyester for natural comfort and advanced temperature regulation. The 17.5 micron merino wool gives the fabric an extra-fine, soft and skin-friendly touch, making it comfortable for close-to-skin wear without the itchy feeling often found in regular wool. Merino wool naturally helps regulate temperature, manage moisture and reduce odor, while 37.5® technology helps remove moisture vapor before it turns into liquid sweat. Together, they keep the wearer drier, fresher and more comfortable in changing conditions. The fine merino wool adds natural warmth, softness and breathability, while 37.5® polyester improves quick-dry performance, durability and active comfort. The lightweight jersey structure makes it ideal for base layers, outdoor tops, travel wear, hiking shirts, running layers and premium performance essentials.
