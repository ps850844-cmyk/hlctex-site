Product information

This sand-washed spacer fabric blends Lenzing Modal, polyester and spandex for a soft, smooth and comfortable handfeel with gentle stretch. Lenzing Modal adds silky softness, breathability and beautiful drape, while polyester improves fabric stability, durability and easy-care performance. Spandex provides flexible stretch and recovery for better comfort and fit. The spacer structure creates a light, airy and slightly cushioned handfeel, giving the fabric a fuller body without feeling heavy. Sand washing further enhances the surface with a soft, peachy touch and a relaxed matte appearance. It is suitable for loungewear, hoodies, casual sets, joggers, travel wear and elevated everyday comfort garments.
