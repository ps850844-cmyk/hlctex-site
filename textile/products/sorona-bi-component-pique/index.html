Product information

This 160GSM Sorona® bi-component pique fabric combines Y-shaped and O-shaped yarn structures to create a naturally soft, micro-brushed handfeel with a lightweight yet lofty body. The special fibre profile builds a bionic porous structure that helps move moisture quickly, delivering fast absorption, rapid wicking and breathable comfort. Sorona® adds natural stretch and recovery without relying heavily on spandex, giving the fabric flexible movement, better shape retention and long-lasting comfort. It also offers a soft touch, lightweight resilience and improved durability, while helping reduce dependence on petroleum-based resources through partially bio-based content. The pique structure enhances breathability, texture and stability, making the fabric feel airy, full and resistant to collapse even at a lighter weight. Suitable for premium polo shirts, golf wear, active casualwear, travel tops and elevated sportswear.
