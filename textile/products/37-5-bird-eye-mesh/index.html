Product information

This 160 gsm 37.5® bird eye mesh uses performance polyester yarn in an open, breathable knit designed for active apparel. The yarn technology helps manage moisture vapour before it condenses into liquid sweat, supporting a drier microclimate during exercise. The bird eye construction improves airflow and ventilation while retaining the durability required for sports tops, polo shirts, golf shirts, team jerseys and performance uniforms.
