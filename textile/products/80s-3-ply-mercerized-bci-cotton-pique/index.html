Product information

This 80S/3 mercerized BCI cotton pique is made with responsible BCI cotton and triple-ply yarn for a refined yet stronger fabric structure. The 80S fine-count yarn gives the fabric a smooth and clean surface, while the 3-ply construction adds better body, stability, shape retention and durability compared with single or double-ply yarns. The pique texture provides natural breathability, subtle texture and a smart casual appearance, making it ideal for premium polo shirts and golf wear. Mercerization enhances fabric luster, color brightness and surface cleanliness, giving the fabric a smoother, more polished and elevated look. Suitable for luxury polo shirts, golf wear, premium collared shirts, resort tops and high-end smart casualwear.
