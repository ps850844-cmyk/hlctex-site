Product information

This 275 gsm 100% BCI cotton interlock is finished with liquid ammonia for a softer, smoother hand, cleaner surface and improved dimensional stability. The stable double-knit construction supports premium T-shirts, polo shirts, underwear, kidswear, sleepwear and loungewear.

Specification and sourcing notes

At 275 gsm, this 100% BCI Cotton Interlock balances the structure of 32S/1 yarn with liquid ammonia. The resulting style HLC-LAC001 is intended for Premium polo shirts, T-shirts, underwear, kidswear, loungewear and sleepwear.

Orders are planned from 300 kg / 300 kg per colour. Standard sample lead time is 15–20 days, and bulk production takes 30–40 days.
