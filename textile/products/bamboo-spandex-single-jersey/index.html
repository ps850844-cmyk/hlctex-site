Product information

This bamboo knit fabric combines 95% Tanboocel bamboo viscose supplied by TANBOOCEL UNION with 5% spandex. Natural breathability and moisture management meet buttery softness and four-way stretch, while waterless dyeing reduces fibre friction for a smooth, silk-like surface. OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 certification supports baby pajamas, zippies, swaddles, sleepwear and loungewear development.

Specification and sourcing notes

Built around 32S + 30D yarn, style HLC-BVJ001 combines 95% bamboo viscose / 5% spandex in a 210 gsm Single jersey. Waterless dyeing is specified to tune its surface and performance for footie, pajamas, Babywear, underwear, sleepwear and loungewear.

The sourcing programme starts from 300 kg / 300 kg per colour. Standard sample lead time is 15–20 days, and bulk production takes 30–40 days.
