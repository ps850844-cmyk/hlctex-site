Product information

This 17.5μm ultra-light merino wool jersey is designed for an exceptionally soft, skin-friendly touch with no itchy feel, making it ideal for close-to-skin performance apparel. The extra-fine merino fibre provides natural temperature regulation, moisture management and odor resistance, helping keep the wearer comfortable in both active and everyday conditions. The ultra-light jersey structure offers breathable comfort, smooth drape and easy movement without bulk. It is suitable for premium base layers, running tops, hiking shirts, travel wear, summer wool T-shirts and lightweight outdoor essentials.
