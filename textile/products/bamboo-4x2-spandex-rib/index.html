Product information

This 4x2 rib fabric combines 95% Tanboocel bamboo viscose supplied by TANBOOCEL UNION with 5% spandex. The rib structure gives better stretch and recovery, a soft textured surface, and a comfortable fit close to the body. Natural breathability and moisture management meet buttery softness, while waterless dyeing helps reduce fibre friction for a smooth, gentle handfeel. OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 certification supports baby pajamas, zippies, cuffs, neckbands, sleepwear and loungewear development.
