Product information

This 80S mercerized SUPIMA cotton interlock is crafted with extra-fine long-staple SUPIMA cotton for a silky-smooth touch, refined surface and luxurious handfeel. The mercerization process enhances natural luster, color brightness and fabric cleanliness, giving the fabric a more polished and premium appearance. Compared with 50S, the 80S yarn creates a finer, lighter and more delicate texture, while still maintaining the soft stability of an interlock structure. SUPIMA cotton provides excellent softness, strength and durability, making the fabric comfortable for close-to-skin wear with an elevated look. Suitable for luxury T-shirts, premium base layers, babywear, sleepwear, loungewear and refined everyday essentials.
