Product information

This 180 gsm 100% BCI cotton jersey uses 50S/2 double-ply yarn for a stable, compact and refined fabric body. Liquid-ammonia finishing creates a smoother surface, better shrinkage control and improved wash appearance while retaining a soft, comfortable touch. Compared with single-yarn jersey, the double-ply construction delivers cleaner texture and stronger structure for premium T-shirts, lightweight sweatshirts, loungewear and sleepwear.
