Product information

This 260GSM RWS merino wool interlock is designed for premium warmth, comfort and natural performance. Made with Responsible Wool Standard certified merino wool, it supports more responsible animal welfare and land management practices. The interlock structure gives the fabric a smooth double-knit surface, fuller body and better shape stability compared with single jersey. Merino wool naturally regulates temperature, manages moisture and resists odor, helping keep the wearer warm, dry and comfortable in changing conditions. With its 260GSM mid-heavy weight, this fabric offers soft warmth, breathable comfort and a stable handfeel, making it suitable for premium base layers, thermal tops, outdoor wear, travel wear, winter loungewear and cold-weather performance garments.

Specification and sourcing notes

Built around 21S yarn, style HLC-W003 combines 100% RWS Merino Wool (18.9 μm) in a 260 gsm interlock. Piece dyeing is specified to tune its surface and performance for premium base layers, thermal tops, outdoor wear, travel wear, winter loungewear and cold-weather performance garments.

The sourcing programme starts from 300 kg / 300 kg per colour. Standard sample lead time is 15–20 days, and bulk production takes 30–40 days.
