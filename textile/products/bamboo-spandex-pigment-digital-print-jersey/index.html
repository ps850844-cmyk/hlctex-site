Product information

This 210 gsm bamboo viscose spandex jersey provides breathable softness, fluid drape and comfortable stretch for printed T-shirts, babywear, pajamas and loungewear. Pigment digital printing supports detailed artwork, multiple colours and flexible small-batch development without conventional screen engraving. HLC offers print sampling, colour coordination, soft-hand finishing, testing and scalable production for overseas apparel programmes.
