Product information

This 200GSM PCM temperature-regulating interlock fabric is developed with PCM derived from natural palm fruit, offering a safe, low-irritation and skin-friendly solution with antibacterial benefits. With a 6J enthalpy value, the fabric provides strong temperature-regulating performance to help maintain the body’s most comfortable surface temperature at around 28-32°C / 30±2°C, making sleep and close-to-skin wear more stable and comfortable. In summer, it delivers an instant cool-touch sensation while providing continuous cooling comfort. In winter, the PCM can actively store and release heat, helping keep the body warmer and more comfortable. The interlock structure gives the fabric a stable, soft and smooth handfeel, making it suitable for sleepwear, loungewear, base layers, babywear and close-to-skin comfort garments.
