Product information

This ultra-fine sand-washed scuba fabric is made with 80S yarn and 0.8D Lenzing Modal, creating an exceptionally soft, smooth and delicate handfeel. The fine 80S yarn gives the fabric a clean and refined surface, while 0.8D Lenzing Modal adds silky softness, beautiful drape and close-to-skin comfort. The scuba structure provides a stable, smooth and slightly cushioned body, while the sand-washing process gives the surface a soft peachy touch and relaxed matte look. Lightweight yet structured, this fabric is suitable for premium loungewear, hoodies, joggers, travel sets, casual tops and elevated everyday comfort garments.
