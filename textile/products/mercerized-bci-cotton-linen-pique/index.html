Product information

This mercerized cotton linen pique combines the comfort of cotton with the natural dry touch and textured character of linen. The classic pique structure provides breathability, shape retention and a smart casual appearance, making it ideal for warm-weather polo shirts and refined casualwear. Mercerization enhances fabric luster, color brightness and surface cleanliness, giving the fabric a smoother and more polished look while maintaining the natural linen texture. The cotton linen blend offers breathable comfort, a crisp handfeel and elevated natural appeal. Suitable for premium polo shirts, golf wear, resort tops, casual collared shirts and summer smart casualwear.
