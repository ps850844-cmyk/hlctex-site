Product information

This polyester spandex single jersey offers outstanding sun protection performance, with T(UVA)AV 0.46, T(UVB)AV 0.2, and an exceptional UPF 500+ rating. The fabric provides strong UV blocking while remaining lightweight and non-see-through compared with regular polyester fabrics. It also features excellent anti-yellowing performance under sunlight, helping maintain a cleaner and more stable appearance after outdoor exposure. In addition, the fabric offers antibacterial protection against three major bacteria groups, supporting better freshness and hygiene for active and outdoor wear. With its smooth stretch handfeel, lightweight structure and high coverage, this polyester spandex jersey is suitable for sun-protection tops, outdoor activewear, base layers, rash guards, training wear and performance lifestyle garments.
