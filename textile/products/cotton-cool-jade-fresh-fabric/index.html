Product information

This fabric is made with Hua Mao Cool Jade-Fresh yarn, blended with 70% cotton and 30% Cool Jade-Fresh fibre for a cool, breathable and skin-friendly handfeel. With a Q-Max value of 0.2, it delivers a clear instant cool-touch sensation on contact with the skin. The Cool Jade-Fresh yarn uses a special Y-shaped and O-shaped cross-section with zinc elements, helping create long-lasting cooling comfort, moisture wicking and a fresher wearing experience. The fibre structure helps absorb moisture, release heat and improve breathability, while also supporting negative ion release for a cleaner skin feel. Cotton adds natural softness and comfort, while Cool Jade-Fresh improves cooling performance, quick drying and lightweight wearability. Suitable for summer T-shirts, base layers, sleepwear, loungewear, kidswear and close-to-skin cooling garments.
