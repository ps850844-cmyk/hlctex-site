Bamboo fabric sourcing for babywear and sleepwear

HLC manufactures bamboo viscose knit fabric with controlled knitting, dyeing, finishing and in-house quality testing. Our bamboo spandex and bamboo cotton jersey fabrics are developed for soft next-to-skin applications including baby pajamas, zippies, bodysuits, underwear, sleepwear and loungewear.

Buyers can review fabric composition, GSM, cuttable width, yarn count, MOQ, lead time and indicative pricing on each product page. Test results and OEKO-TEX information are provided where available to support fabric selection and supplier qualification.

WHY BAMBOO VISCOSE

Softness for sleep. Flexibility for everyday wear.

Bamboo viscose is a regenerated cellulose fibre valued for its smooth hand, fluid drape and comfortable next-to-skin feel. In knitted fabrics, it can be combined with spandex or cotton to balance softness, stretch, recovery and everyday durability.

Soft and smooth against the skin

The fine, smooth surface of bamboo viscose helps create a buttery-soft hand with less of the dry or coarse feel associated with some conventional fibres. This makes bamboo jersey a strong option for baby pajamas, zippies, bodysuits, underwear, sleepwear and sensory-conscious apparel where comfort is central to the design brief.

Breathable comfort and moisture management

Bamboo viscose knit fabric is selected for breathable, moisture-managing comfort in warm sleep environments and close-fitting garments. Fabric construction, weight and finishing all influence the final result, so HLC develops each programme around the required GSM, width, stretch and end use rather than treating every bamboo fabric as identical.

Stretch for sleepwear, recovery for movement

A bamboo viscose spandex single jersey adds four-way flexibility and recovery for fitted pajamas, rompers and loungewear. For buyers who want a slightly more structured hand, bamboo cotton spandex jersey combines bamboo softness and drape with the familiar body and resilience of Better Cotton.

Performance that can be specified and tested

Softness alone is not enough for a commercial fabric programme. HLC supports buyer evaluation with composition, fabric weight, cuttable width, yarn count, MOQ, sample and bulk lead times, together with available test results for dimensional stability, colour fastness, pilling and other requested performance requirements. OEKO-TEX information is supplied where applicable.
