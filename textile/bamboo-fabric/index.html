Why choose HLC?

HLC produces more than 1,800 tonnes of bamboo viscose fabric each year and normally keeps around 80 tonnes of greige fabric and 100 tonnes of yarn ready. This stock gives repeat programmes a faster start and helps us offer competitive pricing and shorter lead times.

Over the past seven years, we have produced more than 13,000 tonnes of bamboo fabric. That experience helps our team manage knitting, dyeing and finishing consistently across babywear, sleepwear, underwear and loungewear programmes.

Why bamboo viscose fabric?

Bamboo viscose is a regenerated cellulose fibre valued for its smooth hand, fluid drape and comfortable next-to-skin feel. It can be blended with spandex or cotton to balance softness, stretch, recovery and everyday durability.

Softness for babywear and sleepwear

Its fine, smooth surface creates a buttery-soft hand suited to baby pajamas, zippies, bodysuits, underwear, sleepwear and sensory-conscious apparel.

Breathable, moisture-managing comfort

Construction, GSM and finishing influence breathability and moisture management, so HLC develops each bamboo knit around the required weight, width, stretch and end use.

Stretch or added structure

Bamboo viscose spandex jersey supports flexible fitted garments. Bamboo cotton spandex jersey adds the familiar body and resilience of Better Cotton.

Performance buyers can verify

Available specifications and test results support evaluation of composition, dimensional stability, colour fastness, pilling, MOQ and lead time. OEKO-TEX information is supplied where applicable.
