Why choose sand-washed knit fabrics?

Soft hand and relaxed drape

Sand washing gives knit fabrics a softer, gently broken-in surface and fluid drape suited to loungewear, sleepwear, tops, dresses and relaxed separates.

Modal, EcoCosy® and polyester blends

HLC develops sand-washed jersey, scuba, pique and French terry using modal, EcoCosy® viscose, polyester and spandex to balance softness, structure and stretch recovery.

Sourcing-ready specifications

Buyers can compare composition, GSM, width, construction, MOQ, lead time, price and available test results before sampling.
