Informations produit

This sand-washed modal polyester French terry combines modal softness with polyester stability in a comfortable loop-back structure. Modal brings a smooth, soft and breathable handfeel, while polyester adds durability, shape retention and easy-care performance. The French terry construction gives the fabric a fuller body, soft inside texture and relaxed comfort, making it ideal for hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers, lounge sets and travel wear. Sand washing adds a soft peach-touch surface and a relaxed matte look, creating a more premium, worn-in comfort compared with regular French terry.

Spécifications et notes d'approvisionnement

Style HLC-SW006 est un French Terry 350 gsm conçu à partir de 66 % de modal Lenzing / 28,3 % de polyester / 5,7 % d'élasthanne avec du fil 40S+100D+30D. Son itinéraire de lavage au sable est sélectionné pour des sweats à capuche, des sweat-shirts, des joggers, des ensembles de détente et des vêtements de voyage.

For purchasing, the working minimum is 300 kg / 300 kg per colour. Standard sample lead time is 15–20 days, and bulk production takes 30–40 days.
