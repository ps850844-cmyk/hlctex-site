Informations produit

Cette page est consacrée au tissu côtelé en bambou et présente bord-côte bambou-élasthanne, maille côtelée en bambou, sa construction, sa composition et son approvisionnement. This 4x2 rib fabric combines 95% Tanboocel bamboo viscose supplied by TANBOOCEL UNION with 5% spandex. The rib structure gives better stretch and recovery, a soft textured surface, and a comfortable fit close to the body. Natural breathability and moisture management meet buttery softness, while waterless dyeing helps reduce fibre friction for a smooth, gentle handfeel. OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 certification supports baby pajamas, zippies, cuffs, neckbands, sleepwear and loungewear development.

tissu côtelé en bambou: Spécifications et approvisionnement

Style HLC-BVR001 is a 235 gsm 4*2 RIB engineered from 95% bamboo viscose / 5% spandex with 32S+30D yarn. Its waterless dyeing route is selected for footie, pajamas, Babywear, underwear, sleepwear and loungewear.

For purchasing, the working minimum is 300 kg / 300 kg per colour. Standard sample lead time is 15–20 days, and bulk production takes 30–40 days.
