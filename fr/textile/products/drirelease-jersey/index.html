Informations produit

This 140GSM drirelease® jersey combines 85% polyester with 15% cotton for lightweight comfort, quick-dry performance and a natural cotton-like touch. The single jersey structure gives the fabric a soft, flexible and breathable handfeel, suitable for close-to-skin activewear and everyday performance garments. drirelease® technology is built into the yarn, not added as a chemical finish, so the moisture-wicking performance is permanent and will not wash out. It helps pull moisture away from the skin and move it to the fabric surface for faster evaporation, keeping the wearer drier and more comfortable. The polyester provides durability, shape retention and fast drying, while cotton adds softness and a more natural feel. Suitable for T-shirts, training tops, running shirts, base layers, gym wear and active lifestyle essentials.

Chez 140 gsm, ce jersey simple 85 % polyester / 15 % coton équilibre la structure du fil 32S drirelease® avec celui teint en pièce.
