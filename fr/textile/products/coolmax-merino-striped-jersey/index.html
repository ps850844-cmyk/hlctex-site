Informations produit

This 120GSM COOLMAX® merino striped jersey combines the quick-dry performance of COOLMAX® with the natural comfort of merino wool in a lightweight breathable structure. COOLMAX® helps move moisture away from the skin and speed up evaporation, keeping the wearer drier during activity, while merino wool adds natural temperature regulation, odor resistance and soft warmth. The striped jersey design gives the fabric a clean visual texture and a more dynamic look, while the 120GSM lightweight construction makes it comfortable for close-to-skin wear without bulk. It is suitable for base layers, running tops, hiking shirts, cycling wear, travel tops and lightweight outdoor activewear.

Spécifications et notes d'approvisionnement

Chez 120 gsm, ce jersey simple 63 % COOLMAX® / 37 % laine mérinos (17,5 μm) équilibre la structure du fil 40S+50D avec une teinture en pièce. Le style résultant HLC-W006 est destiné aux couches de base, aux hauts de course, aux chemises de randonnée, aux vêtements de cyclisme, aux hauts de voyage et aux vêtements de sport légers en plein air.

Orders are planned from 300 kg / 300 kg per colour. Standard sample lead time is 15–20 days, and bulk production takes 30–40 days.
